ABOUT ME

Exploring the intersection of AI and networked systems.

I'm a PhD student in Computer Engineering (Software Engineering) at the University of Tehran, working at the intersection of large language models, computer networks, and distributed systems.

My research interests include intelligent network management, reinforcement learning, federated learning, and the design of adaptive distributed systems. I am advised by Prof. Nasser Yazdani and Prof. Heshaam Faili.

I currently lead the AI engineering team at Adan AI, developing AI-based smart assistant systems.
